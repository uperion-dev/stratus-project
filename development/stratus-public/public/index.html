FROM THE 1.6.7 IN-GAME REVIEW

Water & reflections

Water is one of Stratus’s central design priorities: how it catches the sky, reveals the riverbed and changes as you cross the surface.

At the surface
View angle shapes the balance between reflected scenery and the world beneath the ripples. Missing screen-space scenery blends back to the sky.
Below the waterline
Absorption and scattering depend on the part of the view ray that is actually underwater. The design keeps above-water scenery from receiving a full underwater tint.
Across different worlds
Biome tint, clarity, turbidity and in-game controls let the water respond to its surroundings without replacing Minecraft’s block geometry.

Questions & answers

What is Stratus designed for?

Stratus is an original Minecraft Java shader for Iris. The documented 1.6.7 setup used Minecraft 1.21.11, Iris 1.10.8 and Sodium 0.8.14 on Apple M1/macOS. That is the recorded reference setup, rather than a guarantee for every hardware or mod combination.

Does it replace Minecraft’s textures?

The visual direction preserves original block geometry and texture detail. The shader reads the active game or resource pack’s sun and cloud textures, so those replacements can influence the result.

Where can I adjust the look?

Shader settings in-game expose controls for water, lighting, atmosphere, materials, post-processing and performance. The 1.6.7 reference has 59 controls and four presets, with saved-setting compatibility kept as a project priority.

Which preset should I start with?

Balanced is the middle quality tier: half-resolution water and atmosphere with moderate scenery reflections. Fast favours lower sampling and sky-only reflections; Reference and High spend more work on the effects. Compare them in your own world because hardware, settings and the scene all matter.

Are the screenshots concepts or real game captures?

They are actual Minecraft captures from the documented Stratus 1.6.7 review. The added sunset and underwater frames were captured from the final-code QA build; the main river and sky references include released-build captures. They are saved scenes, rather than a live simulation.

Are upcoming changes already in the shader?

No. The Upcoming page lists requests awaiting a future coding pass. A feature moves into the update logs after implementation and verification; no release dates are promised here.
