# Stratus — editable shader specification

This is the complete, long-term target for Stratus: its intended look, behaviour, construction and checks—not an instruction to rebuild everything on every pass. Put the specific work you want done next in [CHANGES.md](CHANGES.md), then tell the coding chat **“Apply the pending changes.”** Saving either file alone does not start work. In-game option defaults live in shader source. The detailed 1.6.7 snapshot and historical settings inventory remain in [SHADER-SPEC-1.6.7.md](SHADER-SPEC-1.6.7.md).

## Quick edit — major design choices

- Overall look = recognisably vanilla Minecraft: original block geometry and texture detail, with natural lighting, expressive atmosphere and visually impressive water; avoid a uniformly glossy or photorealistic terrain makeover.
- Water look = clear, depth-aware, environment-responsive water with convincing reflections, refraction, ripples, shoreline transitions and underwater visibility, plus a clearly visible adjustable native animated water-texture/biome tint across the whole visible surface, including distant reflective water.
- Reflection approach = scenery where visible, sky fallback where geometry is missing, with no harsh screen-edge artefacts.
- Underwater rule = only the genuinely submerged portion of a view ray gets water tint or fog; banks, air and distant trees above water stay untinted.
- Lower clouds = connected, Minecraft-footprint-based physical clouds with simple vertical shape, broad shading and gentle movement.
- High clouds = separate, bright, wispy cirrus that remains visible even when lower clouds are disabled.
- Mist = layered and varied, with a clear foreground, restrained caves and coherent weather/time changes.
- Daylight = preserve vanilla block colours, texture detail, face/AO shading and lightmap colour/brightness by default. Ordinary entities in vanilla lighting also retain the native two-light directional face shading; preserve separate emissive exceptions. Keep the selectable enhanced sun/sky/ground lighting path and all its tuning controls; vanilla lighting bypasses artistic brightness and colour effects while optional surface-film highlights remain independent.
- Sun = a polished square Minecraft sun with warm HDR core and a local halo; native textured mode remains available.
- Wet materials = restrained adjustable film on exposed porous and non-porous blocks, including metal and glazed materials, in clear weather or rain. Keep absorption darkening/retained dampness off by default, with a separate optional Original dampness control; preserve all-weather/rain-only film choices, exposure/drainage, a uniform material sheen response and a true dry setting. Preserve the underlying vanilla diffuse palette.
- Performance = make the implementation as efficient as measurement allows without removing controls or sacrificing any intended visual feature or quality level.
- Compatibility = keep existing user-facing options, presets, menus, labels and saved-setting behaviour across versions.

### Terrain, lighting, sun and materials

- Enhanced face-lighting design = at full sky exposure, preserve the reproducible 65% vanilla / 35% realistic sky/ground/sun-facing blend; its two components remain independently adjustable.
- Face-lighting controls = retain the separate component controls, overall face contrast and blend controls; setting either overall control to zero bypasses the components in enhanced mode. Keep ambient occlusion and separately adjustable ground warmth. A separate main realistic-lighting switch selects the complete vanilla lighting path without erasing saved component settings.
- Shadows = all current presets have cast shadows disabled; broad directional diffuse lighting must not imply cast-shadow visibility.
- Caves = retain mapped local block light and emission, with controlled brightness and warmth in enhanced mode. Vanilla lighting uses the original game lightmap rather than Stratus ambient/lamp multipliers or invented emission boosts.
- Bloom = restrained; ordinary white blocks and entities must not appear to glow.
- Polished sun texture = sample the active game's/resource pack's square sun through `customTexture.vanillaSun`; keep its silhouette, warm HDR core and a halo local to the sun.
- Sun controls = keep separate core, halo-strength, halo-radius and core-visibility adjustments; do not brighten unrelated objects or alter global bloom as a substitute.
- Native celestial mode = retain native textured sun/moon rendering without a duplicate procedural sun; never redistribute Mojang textures.
- Film sources = selectable all-weather/rain-only response, exposed sky and world-face drainage; use the same film roughness/lobe across eligible material classes. Pore absorption and partial-shelter dampness occur only with optional Original dampness enabled.
- Wet sheen = restore restrained film on exposed leaf/plant and solid faces; suppress inward faces and surfaces hidden behind occluders rather than excluding an entire material. Preserve all-weather/rain-only response, exposure/drainage and independent film-amount/sheen controls; either zero removes film. Film reflectance/roughness must be equal for matched eligible material inputs; native texture, colour, face angle and exposure may still change perceived brightness. Optional Original dampness is separate; all three controls zero is completely dry. Sealed caves remain dry and the vanilla diffuse palette remains. Current rc3 front/back orientation and native depth occlusion do not establish full voxel-neighbour cluster-interior rejection; retain that unfinished requirement and live gate in the active batch.
- Wet-sheen migration = retain SURFACE_DAMPNESS ID/full range as Surface film amount and WET_SHEEN independently. Retain WET_DARKENING ID/range/menu/saved values as a clearly labelled retired no-op, never repurpose it silently. The former coupled absorption path stays retired; ORIGINAL_DAMPNESS independently restores the original local porosity/humidity/weather/shelter/drainage response, default0/off in every preset. Nonporous materials stay unchanged and sealed caves stay dry. This approximates retained dampness locally, not persistent world wetness. Film remains available on nonporous materials. Keep restrained solar film independently of sky film and native diffuse colour; no scenery trace or added pass.
- Colour controls = retain exposure, white balance, saturation, ambient brightness, bloom and lighting controls instead of hard-coding one look. Original dampness may deliberately darken porous diffuse colour only when enabled. Vanilla lighting otherwise bypasses artistic exposure, white balance, saturation, highlight shoulder, bloom and ground warmth while retaining their saved values. Stratus water, clouds and atmosphere remain independently active.

### Water

- Optics = angle-dependent dielectric Fresnel reflection, refraction, ripple normals, depth-dependent absorption/scattering, biome-aware tint, turbidity and clarity; visible geometry stays vanilla. The optional native still/flow atlas pattern and biome colour multiplicatively tint the composed water surface after reflection/transmission mixing, including distant and reflection-dominated water. Preserve native atlas filtering, natural atmospheric fade, sharp reflection detail and opacity; no arbitrary nearby-only cutoff or tint on dry banks/air. Zero strength disables this added surface treatment. Underwater exit/segment attenuation remains independent and unchanged.
- Above-water reflections = support screen-space scenery plus sky; hidden or off-screen geometry is unavailable, so blend misses to sky without severe edge artefacts.
- Fast reflection mode = sky-only; higher modes keep scenery tracing where available.
- Refraction = distortion above water is adjustable. The underwater exit transmits straight through without a Snell window or reflected-scene overlay. In Vanilla water mode, retain the active native animated underside atlas, biome/vertex colour and lightmap, with native alpha scaled by WATER_TEXTURE including smooth intermediate strength and zero. Stratus water mode retains the clear exit. Native valid dark or black colours must not be replaced by a bright biome fallback; use explicit validity/coverage. Preserve nearest-exit depth, transparent ordering and submerged-only fog. Finite rc2 underside/flow views pass; known waterline and complete glass/layering acceptance remain pending.
- Underwater medium = attenuate only the actual submerged ray segment and stop at the nearest visible water exit; missing exits can use clear-sky fallback.
- Water edge cases = keep waterline, shallow/deep river, shore, waterfall, submerged 3D geometry, glass, particles and other transparency coherent.
- Water failure cases = no inverted patches, opaque rectangles, or air/distant banks tinted as if submerged.

### Lower clouds, cirrus and mist

- Vanilla-cloud source = use the running game's/resource-pack cloud alpha footprint via `customTexture.vanillaClouds`; preserve occupied cells and holes with periodic integer texel sampling.
- Lower-cloud topology = extrude the exact horizontal vanilla footprint; no horizontal noise warp, porous erosion or detailed billow field. Height, light, range and tick phase may differ from vanilla.
- Lower-cloud appearance = gentle vertical relief, broad density gradients, self-shadow and height lighting; connected simple forms rather than noisy storm-cloud detail.
- Cloud-control meanings = amount affects opacity without reshaping the footprint; detail affects broad gradients; distortion affects gentle vertical relief only. Existing identifiers and selectable choices remain compatible.
- Cloud integration = continuous midpoint quadrature; split lower-layer traversal at exact vanilla texture-cell boundaries, keep upper-layer steps uniform, and add no temporal jitter or extra pass.
- Cloud safety/range = retain a 1024-segment lower traversal cap and 7000-block outer range; inside the layer, range approaches `max(512, 4*CLOUD_SPAN)`. Check small spans and grazing rays for aliasing/cap effects.
- Cirrus = independent sky-wide wispy veil with natural horizon fade; its own zero/off controls remove it even if lower clouds remain.
- Cirrus density model = optical density `0.045 × CIRRUS_DENSITY`, brighter daylight colour and finite slab/range; not dense storm clouds.
- Mist = spatially varied layers with a clear foreground and restrained cave treatment; preserve weather/time response.
- Thin-cutout fog fallback = below 0.12 cached depth confidence, use full-quality exact-depth atmosphere evaluation and blend back with `smoothstep(0.02, 0.12, confidence)`; include border fog/status effects, never a zero-fog fallback.
- Resource textures = respect current texture-pack replacements and do not bundle Minecraft's cloud or sun images.

### Controls, presets and rendering

- Menu = navigable two-column Water, Lighting, Atmosphere, Materials, camera/colour and Performance categories. Use smaller face/ambient/block-light, lower/high-cloud, cloud-shape, colour and bloom groups, concise labels, useful spacing and English tooltips on every reachable control.
- Control preservation = carry forward every practical shader option, allowed value, preset, menu path and saved-setting identifier; do not hide, narrow or silently repurpose a control for performance.
- Fast preset = lowest effect resolution, sky-only reflections and reduced cloud/mist/refraction sampling.
- Balanced preset = half-resolution water and atmosphere with lighter cloud/mist/refraction sampling than Reference and the same approved sky-only reflection mode; keep all other reflection choices available.
- "Reference (Recommended)" preset = the explicitly approved captured Main visual and quality baseline, currently balanced-resolution water/atmosphere/refraction, sky-only reflections and stronger cloud/mist sampling than Balanced. Exact values live in source. Do not silently lower this approved baseline further for performance.
- High preset = full effect resolution, detailed scenery reflections/refraction and higher cloud sampling, above the approved Reference baseline. All levels remain manually selectable.
- Preset relationship = all current presets disable cast shadows; switching presets may affect both image quality and FPS, so compare actual saved settings rather than assume equivalence.
- Iris architecture = entry wrappers in `shaders/`, shared GLSL in `shaders/core/`, with Nether and End variants.
- Pass order = geometry/translucency → scene resolve → environment → reflection → water composition → atmosphere → bloom/final colour; preserve transparency and depth ordering if reorganised.
- Temporal optimisation = provide switchable prior-frame sky-atmosphere reuse without altering selected fresh-sample quality or frame presentation. Current guarded implementation reuses only one immediately preceding fresh frame in stationary sky; terrain/water, camera/projection changes, stale/reloaded history, slow frames and changed time/weather/status/viewport evaluate freshly. Preserve correct disocclusion and zero/status behaviour, prevent history trails and retain an off switch. Broader live transition/motion and focused performance acceptance remains pending; do not infer gameplay speedup from native timing.
- Optics temporal optimisation = extend temporal optimisation across all reflections and refractions while retaining current quality, controls, sharp colour, correct occlusion and motion. Current rc5 only provides experimental prior-frame distance seeds for water/glass scenery searches and previous rejected shoreline-guard ordering for water refraction; every accepted hit/colour is current-frame. It has no colour-history accumulation and does not skip renderer frames. Cheap sky lookup, thin-glass refraction and the unbent underwater exit remain fresh. Source/GPU/finite river checks do not complete the full request. Measured On adds cost in the manual river scene, so default Off in all presets until a net-efficient implementation and full live acceptance are established.
- Settings implementation = synchronise `options.glsl`, `shaders.properties` and `lang/en_us.lang` when controls change.
- Optimisation = target measured bottlenecks; avoid redundant passes, texture reads and ray steps, and skip invisible-pixel work, while preserving full selected quality and all intended visuals. Retain cloud-envelope skips and invariant mist-lighting hoists. Skip underwater atmosphere reconstruction when its producer guarantees an exact zero contribution; retain identical output and full chosen quality. Single-pass GPU timing does not establish gameplay FPS. Skip unused bloom passes in vanilla lighting and the unused scenery-reflection cache pass in sky-only mode; reject non-water/underwater pixels before fetching interface metadata. Retain projected-ray interval endpoint reuse and invariant dot products, zero-weight mist foreground skips and zero-contribution mist sunlight skips. Aim for the requested approximately 60 FPS on Reference without lowering resolution or quality; keep improvement/target claims pending until matched focused active-game measurements support them.
